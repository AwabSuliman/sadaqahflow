# SadaqahFlow

SadaqahFlow was created for Masjid Al Wasatiyyah Wal Itidaal in Philadelphia to replace scattered forms, spreadsheets, and messages with one reliable food-distribution workflow.

**Live site:** [wasatiyyah-delivery.vercel.app](https://wasatiyyah-delivery.vercel.app/)

- Households submit food requests and follow each delivery from review to completion.
- Approved volunteers claim deliveries, record progress, and report unsuccessful attempts.
- Staff review requests, approve drivers, assign deliveries, manage distribution seasons, and export records.
- Realtime updates and notifications keep recipients, drivers, and staff informed throughout the process.
